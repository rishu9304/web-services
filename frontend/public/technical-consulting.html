Consulting topics

Use consulting when you need a senior engineering perspective before, during, or after development. We cover the technical decisions that determine whether projects succeed, stall, or become expensive to maintain.

  • Product and MVP technical roadmap
  • Technology selection and architecture review
  • Cloud infrastructure and deployment planning
  • API, database, and integration strategy
  • Performance, reliability, and cost review
  • Team structure and hiring guidance for engineering roles
  • Vendor evaluation and build-vs-buy analysis

When consulting helps

Before building

Make architecture, technology, and scope decisions with confidence. Avoid committing to the wrong stack, over-engineering the first version, or underestimating the effort involved. A few hours of consulting can save months of rework.

During development

Get an outside review of progress, code quality, or team velocity. If development feels slow, budgets are growing, or you are unsure whether your team is on track, an independent assessment provides clarity and actionable recommendations.

After launch

Identify performance, scaling, cost, or reliability improvements. Once a product is live, consulting helps you prioritize technical debt, reduce cloud spend, improve response times, and plan the next phase of growth.

What you get

Clarity

A practical explanation of tradeoffs, risks, and recommended next steps. We translate complex technical decisions into language that founders, product managers, and non-technical stakeholders can act on confidently.

Roadmap

A phased plan for what to build, fix, or validate first. We prioritize based on business impact, technical risk, and resource constraints so you know exactly where to focus your time and budget.

Execution support

Help turning recommendations into shipped software or infrastructure improvements. We can stay involved as advisors, review pull requests, guide your team through implementation, or connect you with specialists for specific tasks.

How consulting engagements work

Every engagement starts with a focused call to understand the problem, context, and constraints. We then deliver a written assessment with recommendations, tradeoffs, and a prioritized action plan. Engagements can be one-time advisory sessions for a specific decision, or ongoing retainers where we serve as a fractional technical advisor to your team. There is no long-term commitment required — we scope transparently and work at the pace that fits your needs.

Common consulting scenarios

These are situations where clients typically reach out for technical consulting. If any of these sound familiar, a short conversation can help clarify your options.

  • "We have an idea but don't know how to build it" — MVP scoping and technical roadmap
  • "Our development team is stuck" — architecture review and unblocking guidance
  • "We need to choose a technology stack" — evaluation based on product needs, budget, and team
  • "Our infrastructure costs are too high" — cloud audit and optimization recommendations
  • "We want to improve our release process" — CI/CD pipeline design and DevOps guidance
  • "We need to evaluate a vendor's technical work" — code review and delivery assessment

Frequently asked questions

How much does a consulting engagement cost?

It depends on scope. Short advisory calls can be a few hours. Deeper assessments may take a few days. We scope transparently after an initial call so you know exactly what to expect before committing.

Can consulting lead to a full build?

Yes. Many clients start with consulting and then engage us for development once the plan is clear. Consulting ensures that when you do invest in building, you are building the right thing in the right way.

Do you provide written deliverables?

Yes. All consulting engagements include a written summary with findings, recommendations, and next steps. You will have a document you can share with your team, investors, or stakeholders.

Can you review code or architecture from another team?

Yes. We can audit code quality, architecture decisions, security practices, and deployment infrastructure. This is useful when you want an independent assessment of work done by an internal team or external vendor.

Is consulting available remotely?

Yes. We work remotely with teams across locations using video calls, shared documents, and async communication. Time zones are not a barrier — we schedule around your availability.

What industries have you consulted for?

We have experience across healthcare, education, retail, real estate, manufacturing, startups, and professional services. The technical principles we apply are consistent across industries, and we adapt our recommendations to your specific domain and constraints.
