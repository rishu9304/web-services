Consulting approach

Audit

We review your current architecture, cloud resources, security posture, cost structure, and deployment flow. You get a clear picture of what is working, what is risky, and where the biggest opportunities for improvement are.

Plan

We design a practical path forward covering infrastructure changes, environment strategy, observability setup, and operational processes. The plan is tailored to your team size, budget, and timeline so it can actually be executed.

Implement

We can implement the plan directly or support your team through the rollout with pair sessions, code reviews, and hands-on assistance. Either way, knowledge transfer is built into the process so your team owns the result.

Cloud Infrastructure FAQ

Do you only work with AWS?

No. We can consult on AWS or other cloud platforms depending on your product, team, and business requirements. AWS is our deepest expertise, but we help teams evaluate and work with the platform that fits best.

Can you help reduce cloud costs?

Yes. We review resource usage, architecture choices, scaling patterns, and monitoring to identify cost and reliability improvements. Many teams are overspending on idle or oversized resources without realizing it.

Can you set up CI/CD pipelines?

Yes. We design and implement build, test, and deployment pipelines for reliable releases. This includes branch strategies, automated testing gates, staging environments, and rollback procedures.

Do you handle security?

We implement security basics including access controls, encryption, network policies, and secrets management. For teams with compliance requirements, we help establish the controls and documentation needed to pass audits.

Can you help migrate to the cloud?

Yes. We can plan and execute migrations from on-premise, shared hosting, or between cloud providers. We focus on minimizing downtime and ensuring data integrity throughout the transition.

What size teams do you work with?

We work with teams of all sizes, from solo founders needing infrastructure guidance to engineering teams needing architecture review. Our approach scales to fit your context and budget.
