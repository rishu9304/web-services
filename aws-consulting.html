AWS services we work with

Compute

EC2, ECS, Fargate, and Lambda — matched to the right workload. We help you choose the compute model that fits your application's traffic patterns, scaling needs, and operational capacity. Whether you need always-on instances, container orchestration, or event-driven functions, we design for cost efficiency and maintainability.

Data

RDS, DynamoDB, S3, and ElastiCache for storage and retrieval. We help structure your data layer around the access patterns your application actually needs — relational databases for transactional workloads, document stores for flexible schemas, object storage for files and assets, and caching layers to reduce latency and database load.

Network & CDN

VPC, CloudFront, Route 53, and ALB for performance and security. We set up networking that isolates environments properly, routes traffic efficiently, terminates TLS at the right layer, and uses CDN distribution to serve static assets and API responses closer to your users.

How we engage

Every engagement starts with a discovery call where we learn about your product, team, current infrastructure, and goals. From there, we conduct an architecture review — looking at how services are configured, how deployments work, where risks exist, and what improvements would have the most impact. We then deliver a proposal with clear recommendations and an implementation plan. Depending on your needs, we can operate in an advisory-only capacity — providing guidance and review while your team implements — or we can be hands-on, making changes directly in your AWS environment alongside your engineers.

FAQ

Do I need to be on AWS already?

No, we can plan a migration or a greenfield setup. If you are evaluating cloud providers or moving from another platform, we can help you design the target architecture on AWS from scratch and plan the transition in phases that minimize risk.

Can you help with an AWS cost audit?

Yes, we review usage, rightsizing, reserved instances, and architectural changes that reduce spend. Cost optimization is rarely just about switching instance types — we look at the full picture, including data transfer patterns, storage tiers, idle resources, and whether your architecture is structured in a way that keeps costs predictable as you scale.

Do you manage AWS accounts long-term?

We can provide ongoing support or hand off to your team with documentation. Some clients prefer a retained advisory arrangement where we review changes and stay involved in infrastructure decisions. Others want a focused engagement that ends with a clean handoff, including runbooks, architecture diagrams, and operational documentation.

What if my team already knows AWS?

We can complement your team with architecture reviews, security audits, or specific implementation work. Having AWS experience on your team does not mean outside perspective is unnecessary — we often work with experienced teams on targeted areas like cost optimization, multi-account strategy, or preparing for a significant traffic increase.

Can you help with serverless?

Yes, we help design Lambda-based architectures, API Gateway setups, and event-driven patterns. Serverless can reduce operational overhead significantly, but it also introduces its own complexity around cold starts, observability, and state management. We help you evaluate where serverless fits and where traditional compute is a better choice.

Do you hold AWS certifications?

We bring practical delivery experience. We focus on real-world architecture and production readiness rather than certification credentials. Our recommendations come from building and operating production systems on AWS across different industries, team sizes, and scale requirements.
